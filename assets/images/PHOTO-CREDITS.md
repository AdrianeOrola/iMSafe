# Archive photo provenance (not displayed on the site)

## Current public-domain slideshow

The homepage cycles through seven real historical calamity images, all marked Public domain in Wikimedia Commons source metadata checked on 2026-09-05. These are not AI images or current incident reports. Fire, earthquake, flood and landslide show U.S. events; the typhoon and eruption concern the Philippines, and the tsunami photograph shows Thailand. Source files remain unmodified; the site applies CSS crops, shading, crossfades and slight zoom. No on-page credits or selector bars are rendered.

- `calamity-fire.jpg`: Bitterroot forest fire, Montana, 2000. John McColgan / Bureau of Land Management, Alaska Fire Service. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Deerfire_high_res.jpg
- `calamity-earthquake.jpg`: collapsed freeway following the Northridge earthquake, California, 1994. FEMA News Photo. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Collapsed_freeway_interchange,_1994_Northridge_Earthquake.jpg
- `calamity-flood.jpg`: Missouri floodwater, 2008. Jocelyn Augustino / FEMA. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FEMA_-_34533_-_Flood_waters_in_Missouri.jpg
- `calamity-typhoon.png`: satellite image of Typhoon Haiyan approaching the Philippines, 7 November 2013. NASA LAADS Web; HDF processing by Supportstorm. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Haiyan_2013-11-07_1345Z_(alternate,_borderless).png
- `calamity-volcanic.jpg`: Mount Pinatubo eruption from Clark Air Base, Philippines, 15 June 1991. R. Lapointe / U.S. Air Force, via NOAA/NGDC. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eruption_of_Mount_Pinatubo,_June_15,_1991_(original_resolution).jpg

- `calamity-landslide.jpg`: aerial view of the Oso landslide, Washington, 2014. Mark Reid / USGS. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oso_Landslide_aerial.jpg
- `calamity-tsunami.jpg`: tsunami wave in Ao Nang, Krabi Province, Thailand, 26 December 2004. David Rydevik, released into the public domain by the photographer. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2004-tsunami.jpg
